# The OrcaSlicer settings (src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp) and zslice.
# tools/setting_coverage.py reads this file. The form is the form of
# setting_coverage_cura.txt. A print, printer, or filament key with no
# line here is "missing", with the label of OrcaSlicer as its text. The
# tool sorts the keys of the other blocks of PrintConfig.cpp (resin
# printers, the command line, the G-code variables) by their block.
